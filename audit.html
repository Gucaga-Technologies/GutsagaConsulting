From symptom to root cause

We ask “why?” five times — until we reach the process, not the person.

Example

SymptomEmpty shelf — the product has already arrived from the supplier.
Why?It took three days to reach the sales floor.
Why?Goods receipt stopped on a discrepancy.
Why?Supplier documents did not match the product card.
Why?The product was set up in the system with an error.
Why?There is no rule to check the product card at setup.
= Root cause: a process, not a person.

The same method applies to excess stock, expiry and charts that need attention. Remove the root cause — and the symptom does not come back.

Why daily data

History shows the money. Daily data shows the live cases: on site we ask why the stock is zero or too high today — while people still remember and the documents are at hand. Nobody remembers why a product was missing a month ago. And while the audit runs, you already see where stock is zero or too high today.
