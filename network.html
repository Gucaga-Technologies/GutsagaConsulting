Certification levels

What each level may do

Every licence is valid for 12 months and is renewed on real work with registered clients and the yearly methodology update.

Certified Trainer

Public trainings and book presentations in their country. Audits only together with a founder or Expert.

After: our course, exam and one co-delivered training.

Certified Consultant

Root Cause Audits and plans of changes with clients, independently. In-house team trainings.

After: 2–3 joint audits with a founder.

Expert

Everything above, plus mentoring new partners and leading their joint audits in the region.

After: proven independent work with registered clients, approved by the founders.
